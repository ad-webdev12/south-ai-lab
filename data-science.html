Applied Data Science

This group cleans messy data, finds the patterns in it, and builds models that make predictions. It's the best place to start if you're new, and it's the group for members who want to compete on Kaggle.

Good first group

What you'll learn and build

  • Charts that answer a question about a dataset
  • A model that predicts who survived the Titanic
  • A Kaggle competition entry with your team
  • An analysis of a dataset you pick, presented to the club

Experience needed

None. The fall Foundations sessions start from the first line of Python.

This year

Planned for 2026-27
  • A second Kaggle competition, with teams formed in October
  • A project using data from our own school or town
  • A shared chart style for club presentations

Learning resources

  • Kaggle Learn: PandasCourse, 4 hours

    Loading, filtering, grouping, and combining tables of data.

  • Kaggle Learn: Data VisualizationCourse, 4 hours

    Making line charts, bar charts, heatmaps, and scatter plots with seaborn.

  • NumPy: the absolute basics for beginnersArticle, 2 hours

    The official beginner's guide to arrays, which every ML library is built on.

  • Kaggle Learn: Intro to Machine LearningCourse, 3 hours

    Decision trees, validation, overfitting, and random forests, ending with a competition entry.

  • Machine Learning for EverybodyVideo, 4 hours

    Kylie Ying explains and codes the classic algorithms in Google Colab, one at a time.

  • MLU-ExplainInteractive, 2 hours

    Visual explanations of ideas such as train and test splits, bias and variance, and decision trees.
