Join SAIL

Any High School South student can join. There's no tryout, no cost, and you don't need to know how to code.

When and where

We meet every other Tuesday, 3:00 to 4:00 PM, in Room 700F. You're welcome to come to a meeting before you sign up, and to bring a friend.

Two tracks

Foundations is open sign-up. The Applied Research Division asks for a short project idea, so officers can help you find a team.

Foundations ProgramApplied Research Division
Best for Anyone new to AI or coding. Most ninth and tenth graders start here. Juniors and seniors who are new to programming start here too. Members who have finished Foundations or have built a project before. Mostly juniors and seniors.
What you do Learn Python, work with data, enter a Kaggle competition, and train a neural network by December. Build your own project in the spring. Build a project in a team of two to four, with a research group behind you. Present it at the end of the semester.
Time Meetings, plus about an hour of practice between them. Meetings, plus about two hours a week while your project is running.
Getting in Sign up below. Everyone who signs up is in. Send a short project idea below. An officer will follow up to help shape it and find you a team.

How to join

  1. Join our Google Classroom

    Use code selpcao. Meeting reminders, slides, and notebooks are posted there.

  2. Come to a meeting

    Every other Tuesday, 3:00 to 4:00 PM, Room 700F. Bring a laptop if you have one.

  3. Send us the form

    It tells us your grade, your track, and which research group interests you.

Joining after the year has started? Come to any meeting and tell an officer you're new. Everything we've covered so far is on the Resources page, and we'll help you catch up.

What we ask of members

  • Come to meetings when you can, and tell an officer when you can't.
  • Try the practice exercise for each topic.
  • Present something once a year, even if it's small.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Foundations starts from the first line of Python. The Applied Research Division does expect you to be able to write and fix your own code.

I'm a junior or senior, but I'm new to this. Can I still join?

Yes. Start in Foundations. The tracks are about experience, not grade, and you can move to the Applied Research Division as soon as you're ready.

How much time does it take?

Meetings are an hour every other week. Foundations members should plan on about an hour of practice between meetings. Applied Research teams spend about two hours a week while a project is running.

When do I pick a research group?

You can name one on the form, and you can change it at any time. Applied Research teams work with their group from October. Foundations members join group activities in the second semester.

Do I need my own laptop?

It helps, but it isn't required. Everything runs in a browser, and free tools such as Google Colab and Kaggle provide the computing power.

What if I miss a meeting?

Slides and notebooks are posted on Google Classroom, and the main decks are also linked on the Resources page, so you can catch up.
