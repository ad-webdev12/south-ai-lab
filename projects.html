Titanic survival prediction

Team competition, November 2025, Applied Data Science
Entered by member teams. Starter notebook from Helen Chen's demo repository.

Teams cleaned the passenger data, chose which details to feed the model, trained classifiers with scikit-learn, and submitted predictions to Kaggle's public leaderboard.

Kaggle competitionPreprocessing notebook

Team scores were collected on a form and aren't published here.

Image classification

Workshop exercise, December 2025, Computer Vision
Built by members from a shared starter notebook in Helen Chen's demo repository.

Members built a convolutional neural network in Keras that reads handwritten digits from MNIST. Then they tried the same approach on Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 and changed the architecture to see what helped.

Starter notebookKeras datasets

AI Hub

Member project, April 2025
Built by Aarav Dey.

A website that gathers the club's resources in one place. It was used in meetings during the spring 2025 AI Hub project, where members researched a topic and presented what they found.

Teachable Machine models

Workshop exercise, March 2026, Computer Vision
Built by members at and after the March 3 workshop, alone or with a partner.

Members trained image classifiers in the browser with Teachable Machine. The take-home challenge was to find the most creative use of it, using images, body poses, or sound.

Workshop slidesTeachable Machine

Member models were turned in on Google Classroom and aren't public.
