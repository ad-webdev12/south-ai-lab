Featured projects

Titanic survival prediction

November 2025 · Applied Data Science
Entered by member teams. Starter notebook from Helen Chen's demo repository.

Teams cleaned the passenger data, chose which details to feed the model, trained classifiers with scikit-learn, and submitted predictions to Kaggle's public leaderboard. The chart shows the strongest pattern in the training data: women survived far more often than men, and passengers in first class more often than those in third.

Image classifiers, from digits to clothes to photos

December 2025 · Computer Vision
Built by members from a shared starter notebook in Helen Chen's demo repository.

Members built a convolutional neural network in Keras that reads handwritten digits from MNIST. Then they tried the same approach on two harder datasets, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10, and changed the architecture to see what helped. The same network was less accurate on clothing and photos than on digits.

Teachable Machine models

March 2026 · Computer Vision
Built by members at and after the March 3 workshop, alone or with a partner.

Members trained image classifiers in the browser with Teachable Machine. The take-home challenge was to find the most creative use of it, using images, body poses, or sound, alone or with a partner.
