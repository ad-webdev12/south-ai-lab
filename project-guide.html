Project guide

Ideas to start from

  • Predict something about your own life from data you collect: sleep, screen time, or running times.
  • A classifier that sorts photos from your phone into categories you choose.
  • A model that guesses the genre of a song from its lyrics.
  • A study assistant that answers questions about your own class notes.
  • A game-playing agent for a simple game you know well.
  • A fairness check of a model someone else in the club built.

Find data

  • Kaggle DatasetsTool

    The largest collection, with notebooks showing how other people used each dataset.

  • Hugging Face DatasetsTool

    Text, image, and audio datasets that load in one line of Python.

  • UCI Machine Learning RepositoryTool

    Small, clean, classic datasets. Good for a first project.

  • Keras datasetsTool

    MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10, and a few more, built into Keras.

Share what you made

  • Project README templateTemplate

    Our one-page template: the question, the data, how to run it, results, and limits.

  • GitHub: Hello WorldArticle, about 30 minutes

    Make your first repository.

  • GradioTool

    Turn a Python function into a web demo with a few lines of code.

  • StreamlitTool

    Build a small data app in Python.

  • Hugging Face SpacesTool

    Free hosting for Gradio and Streamlit demos.

Check your model

Before you present, find out who your model works badly for, and write it down.

  • Model CardsTutorial, about 20 minutes

    A Kaggle tutorial on what a model card is and what goes in one.

  • Kaggle Learn: Intro to AI EthicsCourse, about 4 hours

    Lessons with exercises on spotting bias and measuring fairness.

  • AI ExplorablesInteractive, about 1 hour

    Interactive essays from Google on fairness, bias in datasets, and how models fail.
