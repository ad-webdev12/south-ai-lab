About SAIL

SAIL is High School South's AI and machine learning club. We started in 2022 with after-school Python lessons. Now members teach each other Python and machine learning, and then build projects of their own.

You don't need any experience to join. Everything we use is free and runs in a browser, and the slides and notebooks from every meeting are posted for anyone who misses one.

Two ways to join

Pick the one that fits where you are now. You can move from the first to the second whenever you're ready.

Foundations Program

For anyone new to AI or coding

Learn Python, work with real data, enter a Kaggle competition with a team, and train your first neural network by December. In the spring you build a project of your own.

Most ninth and tenth graders start here. So do juniors and seniors who haven't programmed before.

How Foundations works

Applied Research Division

For members ready to run their own project

Work in a team of two to four on a project you choose, with one of our six research groups behind you. Teams present what they built at the end of each semester.

Mostly juniors and seniors, plus anyone who has finished Foundations or built something before.

How the division works

Research groups

Six groups, each focused on one area of AI. Compare them side by side.

  • Applied Data Science Start here

    Find patterns in real datasets and compete on Kaggle.

  • Computer Vision

    Train models that recognize what's in a photo or a video.

  • Natural Language Processing

    Build programs that read, sort, and write text, from spam filters to chatbots.

  • Neural Networks

    Open up a neural network, build one from scratch, and learn the math that makes it work.

  • Agents and Reinforcement Learning

    Make programs that learn by trial and error, and AI assistants that can use tools.

  • AI, Ethics and Society

    Test models for fairness, study privacy and safety, and debate how AI should be used.
