About SAIL

We started in 2022 with after-school Python lessons. Members now teach each other, from a first program up to neural networks, and the slides and notebooks from each meeting are posted afterward.

You don't need any experience, and it's free.

Two ways to join

How to join

Foundations Program

For anyone new to AI or coding

Learn Python, work with real data, enter a Kaggle competition with a team, and train a neural network. In the spring you build a project of your own.

Applied Research Division

For members ready to run their own project

Work in a team of two to four on a project you choose, inside one of the research groups. Teams present at the end of each semester.

Research groups

Compare all six

  • Applied Data Science

    Find patterns in real datasets and compete on Kaggle.

  • Computer Vision

    Train models that recognize what's in a photo or a video.

  • Natural Language Processing

    Build programs that read, sort, and write text, from spam filters to chatbots.

  • Neural Networks

    Build a neural network from scratch and learn the math that makes it work.

  • Agents and Reinforcement Learning

    Programs that learn by trial and error, and LLM-based assistants that use tools.

  • AI, Ethics and Society

    Test models for fairness, study privacy and safety, and discuss how AI should be used.
