Neural Networks

How they learn, and the math behind them

Members build a small network from scratch, watch it learn, and pick up the algebra and calculus that explain why it works.

Join this group

Gradient descent on a loss surface. Click anywhere to start training from that point. Training a network means rolling downhill on a surface like this one, except in millions of dimensions, and different starts can end in different valleys.

This year

This group begins in 2026-27. These are its first projects.

  • A two-layer network written in NumPy, with every gradient checked by hand
  • An experiment comparing how training settings affect learning
  • Member-written notes that explain backpropagation to next year's members

Experience needed

Familiarity with algebra. The rest of the math is taught in sessions.
