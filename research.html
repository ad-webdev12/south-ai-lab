How groups work

The groups are new for 2026-27. Past sessions listed on a group page were whole-club meetings on that topic.

You can name a group when you sign up, and you can switch whenever you like. Applied Research teams work with their group from October. Foundations members join their group's activities in the second semester, once the fall sessions have covered the basics.

Project requirements

Every Applied Research project finishes with the same four things.

  • Share your code with setup instructions.
  • Choose how you will evaluate the project before running experiments.
  • Summarize your results and limitations in a one-page report.
  • Present your work in ten minutes and answer questions.
