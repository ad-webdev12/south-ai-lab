Research groups

The six groups are new for 2026-27. Each one focuses on an area of AI.

GroupWhat members makeExperience needed
Applied Data Science Good first group Charts, prediction models, Kaggle entries None
Computer Vision Image classifiers, webcam demos Can use variables, loops, and functions
Natural Language Processing Text classifiers, similarity search, small chatbots Can use functions, lists, and dictionaries
Neural Networks A network from scratch, training experiments Familiar with algebra
Agents and Reinforcement Learning Game-playing agents, tool-using assistants Can debug a small Python program independently
AI, Ethics and Society Fairness checks, model cards, discussions None

How groups work

Groups work during the regular club meeting, so there is no separate meeting time. You can name a group when you sign up and switch whenever you like.

Applied Research teams work with their group from October. Foundations members join their group's activities in the second semester, once the fall sessions have covered the basics. Past sessions listed on a group page were whole-club meetings on that topic.

Project requirements

  • Share your code with setup instructions.
  • Choose how you will evaluate the project before running experiments.
  • Summarize your results and limitations in a one-page report.
  • Present your work in ten minutes and answer questions.
