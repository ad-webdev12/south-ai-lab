Becoming an officer

Any member can apply. Each research group also gets a group lead, named in October.

  1. April

    The officer interest form opens on Google Classroom.

  2. May

    Applications close and the current officers meet with applicants.

  3. June

    The new team is announced at the last meeting of the year.
