Roles

Directors
Set the session schedule, teach or assign each session, and decide what is presented at the end of the term. Three share the position so that no one person carries the year.
Deputy directors
Run instruction and supervise project teams. Most weekly sessions are taught by a deputy director or a group lead.
Group leads
One for each research group, appointed in the fall. A lead maintains the reading list, approves proposals, and reports on the group at the end of term.
Records and communications
Keeps the meeting record, posts announcements, and maintains the archive of past sessions and materials.
Junior officer
Usually an underclassman. Covers outreach, recruitment, and arranging guest speakers.

Selection

When
Applications in April, interviews in May
Who can apply
Any member in good standing
What counts
Attendance, finished work, and teaching other members
Term
One academic year
